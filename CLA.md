# Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

> **Status: starting text.** It follows the structure of the Apache Individual and Corporate CLAs, adapted to a project that wants
> to keep the freedom to relicense. It is **not legal advice** and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. It must be reviewed by a
> lawyer before any commercial use of the project.

Thank you for your interest in LivingPaper. This agreement is between **you** and **AntoineYREM** (the "Maintainer"). It
clarifies the rights on what you contribute, so that the Maintainer can distribute the project, now and later, under the AGPL-3.0
and under other terms, for example in a commercial edition.

**You keep the ownership of your contribution.** You are only giving the Maintainer the rights listed below.

## 1. Definitions

- **"Contribution"**: any code, documentation, translation, example file or other work that you intentionally submit to the
  project (a pull request, a patch, an issue with code), including every modification of it.
- **"You"**: the individual who signs, or the legal entity on whose behalf a Contribution is made (see article 8).

irrevocable license** to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform, **sublicense** and
distribute your Contribution and its derivative works, **under any license terms**, including proprietary terms.

In particular, you agree that the Maintainer may **relicense** your Contribution: the project is distributed today under the
AGPL-3.0, and the Maintainer may distribute it, in whole or in part, under other terms, whether open source or not.

You grant the Maintainer and the people who receive the project a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, free, irrevocable

## 4. What you promise

- You are legally entitled to grant these licenses. If your employer has rights on your work, you have their permission (or a
  waiver), or they sign article 8.
- Each Contribution is **your original creation**, or you say who made it and under which license when you submit it. You do not

- If you used an AI tool to write a Contribution, you have read it, you understand it and you are responsible for it, as if you

- Your Contribution holds no secret, no personal data of anyone, and no text under copyright that you have no right to share.

## 5. No obligation, no warranty

You are not required to provide support. Unless the law or a written agreement says otherwise, you provide your Contribution
**as is**, without warranty of any kind. The Maintainer is not required to use your Contribution, and may remove it.

## 6. Credit

The Maintainer keeps the history of the repository, which shows your name or pseudonym next to your Contribution. Contributors
keep their authorship. The Maintainer is not required to keep a name in any other place.

## 7. How to sign

A bot (CLA Assistant) asks you, in your first pull request, to sign by posting a comment: *"I have read the CLA Document and I
hereby sign the CLA"*. Your GitHub identity is recorded with the date. The signature covers your present and future
Contributions, until you notify the Maintainer in writing that you stop (this does not affect Contributions already made).

## 8. On behalf of a company

If you contribute as part of your job, or for an entity, the entity must also agree to this text. Open an issue or write to the
Maintainer **before** your first pull request, so that a corporate agreement is signed by someone who can bind the entity. The
Maintainer then keeps the list of the people the entity authorises to contribute.

## 9. Law and changes

This agreement is governed by the law of the country where the Maintainer lives, unless a mandatory rule says otherwise. The
Maintainer may publish a new version of this text; it applies to the Contributions made after you accept it.
